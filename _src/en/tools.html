A simple workflow

  1. Research what already works for your topic with the Thumbnail Downloader and our guide to analyzing competitor thumbnails.
  2. Check your design for contrast, clutter and small-size readability with the Thumbnail Analyzer.
  3. Review the judgment calls with the Thumbnail Checklist.
  4. Write and check the title with the Title Generator.
  5. See it in context with the Feed Preview, next to real videos from your niche.
  6. Plan your income with the Revenue Calculator.
