Key takeaways

  • Watch hours = views × average view duration. Improving either one works — duration is often easier.
  • Only public long-form watch time counts. Shorts feed, private, unlisted and deleted videos don't.
  • 4,000 hours in a year is about 11 hours a day; 8,000 is about 22 hours a day.
  • Never buy watch time: it's filtered out and can get a channel rejected.

The count is a rolling window: the last 12 months today, the last 365 days under the 2027 rules. Watch time older than that drops out. See monetization requirements for the full rules and dates.

Do the math for your channel first

The fastest way to know what to focus on is to put your numbers in the watch hours calculator. The formula is simple:

Watch hours per day = views per day × average view duration (minutes) ÷ 60

Notice how much average view duration matters: going from 2 to 6 minutes cuts the daily views you need by two thirds.

Increase average view duration

  • Start with the payoff. Confirm in the first seconds that the viewer is in the right place — show the result or restate the question from the title. Long intros are the most common drop-off point.
  • Structure the video. Clear steps, numbered sections and chapters give people reasons to keep watching (and let them skip to what they want instead of leaving).
  • Cut dead time. Remove repetition, long pauses and tangents. A tighter 9-minute video usually beats a padded 12-minute one.
  • Make videos as long as the topic deserves. Topics with real depth — tutorials, reviews, explainers, walkthroughs — naturally hold attention longer.
  • Study the retention graph in YouTube Studio for each upload and fix what makes people leave. Our guide on CTR vs. watch time explains how to read it.

Increase views from the right people

  • Target searchable topics. Search-driven videos keep collecting views for months, which keeps your rolling total up. See YouTube SEO.
  • Improve packaging. A better thumbnail and title on a video that already gets impressions is the quickest win. Start with the thumbnail guide and the thumbnail checklist.
  • Publish consistently. A predictable schedule builds returning viewers, who watch longer than first-time visitors.
  • Connect your videos. Use end screens, cards and playlists so one view leads to the next. Playlists of related videos are one of the simplest ways to increase session watch time.

Use live streams and Shorts wisely

  • Live streams: a public stream saved as a video counts toward watch hours, and streams often run long. They work best when your audience already knows you.
  • Shorts: they don't add watch hours, but they can bring new subscribers who then watch your long videos. If Shorts are your main format, consider the Shorts path (10M views in 90 days, 20M for new applicants from February 2027) instead.

What not to do

  • Don't buy views, "watch hour packages" or subscribers. They violate YouTube's fake engagement policy, are filtered out of the count, and can lead to rejection or termination.
  • Don't use view-for-view groups or loop videos. The watch time is low quality and can be treated as artificial.
  • Don't make old videos private before you're accepted unless you have to: their watch time stops counting.
  • Don't upload reused content to fill hours. Even if you reach the numbers, reviewers can reject channels built on reused material.

A 90-day plan

  1. Week 1: run the numbers in the calculator, find your 5 best videos by watch time and study why they work.
  2. Weeks 2–4: improve thumbnails and titles on videos with impressions but low CTR; add chapters and end screens everywhere.
  3. Weeks 4–12: publish on a fixed schedule, mostly on topics similar to your best performers, and review each video's retention graph within a week of publishing.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get 4,000 watch hours?

It depends entirely on views and duration. A channel with 165 daily views at 4 minutes average reaches it in about a year; use the calculator for your own numbers.

Do my own views count?

Normal viewing from real accounts counts, but repeatedly watching your own videos to inflate numbers isn't legitimate and won't meaningfully change the total.

Should I wait for 8,000 hours or aim to qualify before February 2027?

If you're close to 4,000 hours and 1,000 subscribers, prioritize reaching them and applying before February 1, 2027. If you're far away, plan for 8,000.
