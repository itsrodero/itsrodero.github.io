    YouTube's rules for chapters

    Chapters appear on the progress bar and let viewers jump to a section. YouTube creates them from timestamps in your description, but only when these rules are met:

    1. The first timestamp is 0:00. This is the most common reason chapters don't show up.
    2. There are at least three timestamps, listed in ascending order.
    3. Each chapter lasts at least 10 seconds.

    Chapters can take a while to appear after you save the description. You can also let YouTube generate automatic chapters in YouTube Studio, but writing your own gives you control over the titles.

    What the generator accepts

    You can paste timestamps in almost any common format: 1:12 Title, Title 1:12, (01:12) Title, 1:12 - Title, or hour-long videos like 1:02:30 Title. The output uses the format YouTube recognizes: one chapter per line, time first.

    Writing chapter titles that help

    • Describe the content, not the format: "Choosing a microphone" beats "Part 2".
    • Use the words people search for. Chapters can appear in Google search results as "key moments", so clear titles can bring extra visits.
    • Keep them short so they fit on the progress bar on phones.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why aren't my chapters showing?

    Check that the first timestamp is 0:00, that you have at least three, that they're in order and that each chapter is at least 10 seconds long. Chapters may also be turned off in the video's settings, or take some time to appear.

    Do chapters help SEO?

    They make videos easier to navigate and can appear as key moments in Google search. They won't rescue a video people don't want to watch, but they do help viewers find the part they came for.

    Is my text stored?

    Your last timestamps are saved only in your browser so you don't lose them. Nothing is sent to us.
